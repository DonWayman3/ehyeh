    Study Assistant

    An opt-in study helper. It answers short study questions — it never writes your sermon.

    Enable the assistant

    The assistant needs a language-model key you provide. Nothing here calls any service until you enable it and type a question.

    Kept only in this tab's session memory — never saved to disk, never sent anywhere except the endpoint below, only when you ask a question.

    Use a free endpoint (for example OpenRouter's free models, or a local model at http://localhost:11434/v1/chat/completions). Never a paid call without your say-so.

    Ask a study question

    Ask about the text you're studying. Requests for sermon wording, illustrations, or prayers will be declined.
